Our Story

A leading name in foreign exchange, built on reliability

FTH Wire Services is a leading provider of foreign exchange services, built over years of experience helping students, families, and businesses send money across borders. What began with a focus on university fee payments from India has grown into a full suite of services: currency exchange, forex travel cards, and outward remittance.

We work as an RBI-authorized Dealer – Category II, which means every transfer you make with us moves through regulated, compliant channels — verified KYC, transparent rates, and full traceability from booking to credit.

Our team of experienced and knowledgeable professionals is committed to providing a personalized, stress-free experience, tailoring solutions to what you actually need — whether that's a one-time tuition payment or an ongoing corporate remittance arrangement.

What We Stand For

The principles behind every transfer

  • Trust & compliance — Every transfer runs through RBI-regulated rails as an Authorized Dealer – Category II, with full KYC verification.
  • Transparent pricing — Rates that beat the bank, shown upfront — no hidden mark-ups buried in the exchange rate.
