Our Story

A trusted partner for every kind of transfer

We work with students, travellers, families and businesses across their foreign exchange and international transfer needs. From exchanging foreign currency to paying university fees and sending money abroad, we help customers find competitive rates and navigate the process from start to finish.

We work as an RBI-authorized Dealer – Category II, which means every transfer you make with us moves through regulated, compliant channels — verified KYC, transparent rates, and full traceability from booking to credit.
