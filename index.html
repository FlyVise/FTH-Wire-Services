Get Ready

Documents you'll need

For a university fee payment, keep the following documents ready. The sender can be either the student or a parent, provided the funds are transferred from the sender's own bank account.

  • University letter or prospectus showing the amount payable
  • Student's Indian passport
  • Sender's ID proof — Aadhaar, driving licence, voter ID or passport
  • Sender's PAN card
