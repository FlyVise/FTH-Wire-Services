Get Ready

Documents required for university fee payments

Keep these documents ready. The sender may be the student or a parent, as long as the funds are transferred from the sender's own bank account.

  • University letter or prospectus showing the amount payable
  • Student's Indian passport
  • Sender's ID proof — Aadhaar, driving licence, voter ID or passport
  • Sender's PAN card

You'll also need the university's banking details, including:

  • University name and address
  • University bank name and address
  • University account number
  • SWIFT code
  • Routing number or transit code

A practical way to manage your money abroad

You can use a forex card for purchases at merchant outlets, online transactions and cash withdrawals at ATMs. Unlike carrying a large amount of physical currency, a forex card can be reloaded when required and offers additional protection if your cash is lost or stolen.

Plan Ahead

How much foreign currency should you carry abroad?

There isn't one amount that works for every traveller. A practical approach is to carry a small amount of cash for places where cards may not be accepted, keep a credit or debit card available for larger purchases and ATM withdrawals, and use a prepaid forex card for your planned foreign currency expenses.

A forex card can also allow you to lock in a specific amount of foreign currency at the applicable rate, helping you manage exposure to currency fluctuations.

Cash limits and regulations vary by country, so check the applicable rules with your bank or a forex professional before travelling.

Choose Your Card

Two ways to carry your currency

Forex cards generally fall into two broad categories.

Single-Currency Card

As the name suggests, a single-currency card is loaded with one currency. It can be a suitable option if you're travelling or studying in one country.

Multi-Currency Card

A multi-currency card lets you load multiple currencies onto the same card. This can be useful if your travel plans involve more than one country.

Depending on the card, additional features may include travel insurance, purchase protection, emergency assistance and loyalty rewards. Before choosing a card, compare the applicable fees, features and terms and conditions rather than looking at the exchange rate alone.
